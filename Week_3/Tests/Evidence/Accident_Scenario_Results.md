# Accident Detection: Scenario Results (SIMULATED data)

> **All samples are generated by software** (`Firmware/Accident_Detection/sim_scenarios.cpp`, 50 Hz, GPS speed at 1 Hz, deterministic noise). Thresholds are **placeholders**.
> These results show how the detector logic behaves on made-up data. They are **not** evidence of real accident-detection performance, and they say nothing about real sensor behaviour.
> Source: replay harness `Tests/Test_Suites/tests_accident.cpp` (host build of the same code). Re-run it in Wokwi and use your own output as evidence.

Columns: candidates = stage 1 triggers; confirmed = accident classified after the confirmation window; rejected = candidate rejected. "Ideal" is my judgement of whether a good detector should confirm an accident in that simulated case.

## Trigger counts and false positives
- **Run A:** 9 candidate triggers, 5 confirmed, 0 missed accidents, **2 false positives**.
- **Run B (jerk on):** 10 candidate triggers, 5 confirmed. Jerk adds the single-sample pothole as a candidate; the later stages reject it.

## The two false positives (important)
1. **bump_then_hard_stop:** a bump followed, within the 6 s confirmation window, by a hard stop to 0 km/h. The pipeline sees an impact, then a speed drop and stillness, and confirms. A normal emergency stop right after a bump looks like a crash. Possible mitigations (not implemented): require a larger impact, require an orientation change or gyro spike, require the speed drop to start within a short time of the impact, or add a driver cancel step.
2. **bump_gps_missing_smooth:** with no GPS speed and a very smooth road, steady driving looks "still" to the IMU (almost no vibration), so a bump is confirmed with LOW CONFIDENCE. Without GPS speed the stillness check cannot tell steady cruising from being stopped.

## Other behaviours worth noting
- **Rough road without GPS (bump_gps_missing_rough):** road vibration keeps the IMU from looking still, so the candidate is rejected. This depends on the *assumed* simulated vibration level (0.12 g), which is not a measurement.
- **Parked knock (parked_door_slam):** rejected because GPS speed shows the vehicle was not moving before the impact.
- **Minor contact then driving on (minor_contact_drive):** rejected because the vehicle keeps moving. Whether this should alert is a policy question.
- **Rollover-like:** confirmed with a 90 degree orientation change and a gyro spike (442 deg/s). **Frontal impact:** confirmed with no orientation change, so requiring orientation by default would miss it (`requireOrientation` is off by default).
- **Hard braking, cornering and normal driving** produced no candidates, because their magnitudes stay near 1 g (below the 3 g placeholder threshold).

## What these results do NOT show
Real vehicle dynamics, real sensor noise and mounting, sensor saturation in real impacts, real GPS speed latency, real crash signatures, or any false-positive or false-negative rate. Real data is needed before any threshold is treated as meaningful.
